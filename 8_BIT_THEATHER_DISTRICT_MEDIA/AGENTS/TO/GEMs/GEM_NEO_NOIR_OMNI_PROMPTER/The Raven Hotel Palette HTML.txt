The Raven Hotel Palette (Altered Carbon)
________________
An HTML-formatted analysis of the atmospheric cyberpunk colors present in the scene visuals.




Neon Orange / Glowing Amber
#FF7B39
The prominent color used for "The Raven Hotel" neon sign and the internal window grids.


Deep Shadow / Dark Charcoal
#12161A
The dominant background tone representing the sleek, wet streets, shadows, and building structures.


Cyberpunk Neon Green
#00FF66
An electric, vibrant accent color seen reflecting off the wet pavement and glowing from terminal screens.


Muted Teal / Deep Aquamarine
#1D3D3D
Found in the atmospheric fog, overhead structural lighting, and ambient sky glow.


Soft Warm White / Light Cream
#E6DFD3
The color of the entrance doorway lighting where characters are standing, serving as a focal point.
