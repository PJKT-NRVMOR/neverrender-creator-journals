## 3. Mandatory Glassworks & Metal Craft Rules (Every Boss Portrait & Banner)

Every `Project Astackwork Neon` asset MUST exhibit real physical craftsmanship:
1. **The Glassworks (Hand-Bent Borosilicate Tubing):**
   - Visible clear glass wall thickness and curved torch-bent elbows (no flat digital laser lines floating in thin air).
   - **Matte-black blockout paint** masking non-luminous crossover bends between tube sections.
   - **Dual-Gas Physics:** Outer primary architectural glass ring/frame filled with pure **`Ne-10` burnt-sienna vermilion (`#E8451E` -> `#FF6B2B`)**, paired with an inner **Mercury-Argon aqua-cyan (`#2CE5F6`)** or agent-signature phosphor glass tube.
2. **The Metal (Glassworks Requires Metal):**
   - Machined **antique brass tube-support standoffs**, knurled copper clamping collars, **glazed black/white porcelain high-voltage electrode boots**, braided copper transformer cables, and riveted dark petrol-iron mounting rails.
3. **Vogue Steampunk Tailoring (Editorial Restraint):**
   - Calm, sculptural gallery softbox lighting inspired by high-fashion neo-classical steampunk (weathered petrol-slate backdrop, matte porcelain/velvet/wool/gloss leather, bespoke hammered brass optical bezels, smoked glass lenses, etched cuff hardware). Zero costume-party plastic gears glued onto hats.
4. **Finishing Pipeline Lock:**
   - Generate clean `2K` plates in Firefly / Nano Banana 2 (`<= 750` chars, zero salad-bowl bloat) -> finish through Matthew's **Dialed-Back Frank Moth Baseline** Lightroom preset to lock `#06090E` obsidian shadows and true `640.2 nm` vermilion ignition.

## 6. Certified Finishing Grade: `astackwork-neon.xmp` (Lightroom v19.0 / ACR 15.4)

Archived permanently at `01-vision-and-color-science/xmp-calibration/astackwork-neon.xmp` (verified on Take-1 Boss Master `wurstner-astackwork-neon (3x4)`):
- **Surface Micro-Contrast:** `Texture +26`, `Clarity +15`, `Dehaze +22`, `ParametricHighlights -36`, `ColorGradeGlobalLum -46`, `PostCropVignette -17` (destroys synthetic AI studio sheen and locks tactile leather/brass/ink grain).
- **Color Split & Blue-Spill Kill:** Shadows `Hue 133 / Sat 24` (oxidized petrol-emerald), Highlights `Hue 35 / Sat 23` (warm antique brass), Midtones `Hue 264 / Sat 19`, Global `Hue 114 / Sat 9`, **`Blue Saturation -36`** (eliminates artificial digital blue spill while preserving true Petrol-Teal and `Ne-10` burnt-sienna vermilion).
- **Physical Optics & Analog Film Print:** `Glow 13` (borosilicate glass halation), `LensBlur 31` with **`BokehAspect -100`** (true anamorphic oval depth separation), `Grain 22 / Size 25 / Roughness 50`, and matte darkroom tone curve (`0 -> 6`, `255 -> 245`).
