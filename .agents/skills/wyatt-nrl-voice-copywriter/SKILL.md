## Strict AI-Tell Sanitization Rules (The Zero-Slop Filter)

1. **Zero Em-Dashes or En-Dashes**:
   - Never output the em-dash or en-dash character anywhere in prose, bullet lists, email subjects, or table cells.
   - Replace with commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or standard ASCII hyphens (`-`) / double-slashes (`//`).
2. **Zero Bracketed Citations or NotebookLM Artifacts**:
   - Strip all inline citation brackets (`[1]`, `[2]`, `[33, 34]`, `[cite: 1, 2]`) that leak from NotebookLM, RAG pipelines, or search syntheses.
3. **Zero Corporate AI Buzzwords**:
   - Banish "delve", "testament to", "tapestry", "symphony", "beacon", "unlock", "elevate", "synergistic", "holistic", "paradigm", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced digital landscape", "moreover", "furthermore", "crucial", "foster", and "It's not just X, it's Y".
   - In visual prompts, replace vague quality tokens ("photorealistic", "hyperdetailed", "stunning", "masterpiece", "4K/8K") with concrete optical and material nouns ("70mm anamorphic prime", "wet reflective asphalt", "12% bottom luma crush").
4. **Client Punctuation Alignment**:
   - When ghostwriting for or emailing Melissa Chapman, enforce her zero em-dash rule, balanced two-sentence paragraph option for executive pitches, and no Oxford commas before `and` in simple lists.
5. **Google Docs Export URL Preservation**:
   - When archiving reports to Google Docs, format links as `* [Title](URL) - URL` so raw URLs survive export.
